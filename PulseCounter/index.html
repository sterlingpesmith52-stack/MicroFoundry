    How to count your pulse

    Find your pulse on the inside of your wrist or the side of your neck, using two fingers rather than your thumb.

    Choose 15, 30 or 60 seconds, then tap the big button once for every beat. The clock starts on your first tap.

    When time is up, PulseCounter works out your beats per minute. Switch to Breathing to count breaths the same way.

    Questions

    What is a normal resting heart rate?

    For most adults, a resting heart rate between 60 and 100 beats per minute is considered typical. Fit people and some medications can make it lower.

    What is a normal breathing rate?

    For adults at rest, about 12 to 20 breaths per minute is typical.

    Is PulseCounter a medical device?

    No. It's for everyday tracking only. If a reading worries you, talk to a nurse or doctor.
