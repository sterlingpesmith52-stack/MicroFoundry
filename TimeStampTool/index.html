Copy the date and time in one tap

TimeStampTool shows the current date and time in five formats, from "October 20, 2026 at 9:58 AM" to a sortable 2026-10-20 09:58.

Tap any format to copy it, then paste it into a note, a log, a spreadsheet or a message.

Tap Update to now to refresh the time.

Questions

Which format should I use for spreadsheets?

Use Sortable (year-month-day). It sorts correctly in any spreadsheet.

Does it use my time zone?

Yes. The time comes from your phone, so it matches your local time.
