MicroNote

Your note saves as you type and stays on this phone.

0 characters

A notepad that saves itself

Start typing and MicroNote saves your note as you go. There's no save button to forget and no account to create.

Tap Add time to stamp the date and time where your cursor is, which is handy for logs and to-do lists.

Your note stays on this phone. Tap Copy to move it into an email or message.

Questions

Where is my note stored?

On your phone only. Nobody else can see it, and it isn't uploaded anywhere.

Will my note still be there tomorrow?

Yes, as long as you open MicroNote in the same browser and don't clear its data.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Add MicroFoundry to your home screen and MicroNote works without internet.
