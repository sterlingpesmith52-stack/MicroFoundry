QuickCounter

Tap the big button to count. Your count is kept if you leave the page.

How to use this tally counter

Tap the big button once for each thing you count: people coming through a door, laps, stitches, reps or birds at the feeder.

Your count is saved on your phone automatically, so you can close the page and pick up where you left off.

Tap −1 if you tap one too many, and Copy to paste the total into a message or note.

Questions

Is QuickCounter free?

Yes. You can try every MicroFoundry tool free, with no sign-up.

Will I lose my count if I close the page?

No. The count is kept on your phone until you tap Reset.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Once you've opened it, add MicroFoundry to your home screen and it works offline.
