How Decider picks for you

Type your options, one per line: places to eat, movies to watch, chores to tackle first.

Tap Choose for me and Decider picks one completely at random, so every option has an equal chance.

Your list is saved, so you can ask again next time without retyping it.

Questions

Is the choice really random?

Yes. Every option on your list has the same chance of being picked.

Can I use it to settle an argument?

That's what it's for. Agree to go with Decider's pick before you tap.

How many options can I add?

As many as you like, as long as there are at least two.
