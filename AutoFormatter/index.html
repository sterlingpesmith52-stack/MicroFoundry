How to clean up messy text

Paste text that came out badly: an email with lines broken in odd places, text copied from a PDF, or notes full of double spaces.

Tap Format. AutoFormatter joins lines broken mid-sentence, removes extra spaces and blank lines, and keeps your paragraphs.

Turn on the extra options to straighten curly quotes or capitalize the start of sentences, then tap Copy result.

Questions

How do I remove line breaks from text copied from a PDF?

Paste it into AutoFormatter with "Join lines broken mid-sentence" turned on, then tap Format.

Will it change my words?

No. It only fixes spacing, line breaks and, if you choose, quotes and capital letters.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Formatting happens on your phone, and your text never leaves it.
